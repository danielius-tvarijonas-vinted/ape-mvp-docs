APE documentation

APE is an agentic prototyping environment.

If you've never opened it, start with Introduction. Otherwise everything is below.

Getting started​

  • Introduction – what APE is, what it builds against, and what it deliberately won't do.
  • Getting access – two clicks and one command.
  • Quick start – from nothing to a working prototype in five minutes.

Concepts​

  • Sessions – a container, a branch, and an agent, and what happens when you walk away.
  • How your work is saved – every prompt is a commit, and nothing reaches master without a pull request.
  • Templates and projects – sandbox scaffolds, real repos, and the ones you make yourself.

The workspace​

  • The session screen – a tour of the two panes, the header, and everything behind Advanced.
  • Prompting the agent – what to say, how big asks get planned, and the three things Enter can do.
  • The live preview – the real app, running, with a URL bar.
  • Pointing at things – clicking a thing instead of describing it.
  • Files and history – seeing what changed, editing it yourself, and going back.

Working with others​

  • Sharing – who can see your prototype, and who can change it.
  • Comments and notes – feedback attached to a thing, and messages meant for a person.

Shipping​

  • Pull requests – from a branch to something reviewable.
  • Publishing – a public URL for a prototype, and keeping it current.

Reference​

  • Reference – statuses, limits, costs, and every aiw command.
  • Troubleshooting – what to do when it doesn't work.
  • CLI and SSH onboarding – for engineers who want to work in Cursor over SSH.
