Sharing

Two levels: people who can look, and people who can build.

Share a link​

Click Share in the header to copy a link.

Anyone on the VPN who opens it gets a read-only view. They can:

  • Click through the whole prototype
  • Change device sizes and navigate routes
  • Leave comments anchored to specific elements

They can't prompt the agent, edit files, publish, or open a pull request.

They don't need to be added to anything, and they don't need to have used APE before. The link is the permission – anyone who has it, has access. Treat it accordingly.

If you copy the link from the preview's URL bar instead of the header, it opens on the route you're currently looking at. Better than sending someone to the home page with instructions on where to click.

Invite a collaborator​

In the same dialog, search by name or email and add someone. Collaborators can do everything you can: prompt the agent, edit files, open a pull request, publish.

They'll find the session under Shared with me in their prototypes list.

The dialog shows everyone with access under Has access, marked Owner or Editor. Remove someone from the same place.

Working at the same time​

Nothing stops two people using a session together, and it's designed for it.

  • Avatars in the header show who's viewing right now.
  • Typing indicators appear in the chat.
  • Everything is live – messages, the agent's progress, file changes, and comments all arrive without a refresh.

Two people prompting at once is fine. The second prompt queues behind the first rather than colliding with it, and the queue shows who sent what.

If you need to tell your collaborator something without starting an agent run, use a note – see Comments and notes.

Which to use​

You wantUse
Feedback on a prototypeShare link. Reviewers can comment without edit access.
Someone to build with youCollaborator.
To show it in a meetingShare link, copied from the URL bar on the right route.
To send it outside the VPNNeither – publish it.

A share link only works for people with a Vinted account who are on backup-vinted-vpn – worth mentioning when you send one, since being on Vinted's other VPN looks identical to the link being broken. For anything wider, publishing gives you a genuinely public URL.
